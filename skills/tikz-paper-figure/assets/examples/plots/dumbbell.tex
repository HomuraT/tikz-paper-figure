% Candidate 5: dumbbell (paired dot) plot. One row per benchmark, grey dot = baseline, blue dot = ours, the gain
% printed after the pair; rows sorted by gain, a regression in orange. The whisker at the blue dot is the 95%
% CI of the gain (ours minus baseline), not of our score: a whisker that reaches back to the grey dot means the
% gain does not exclude zero. Two separate CIs compared by eye would not say that (Wilke, Visualizing the
% uncertainty of point estimates). Replaces a grouped bar chart when the question is "how much did we gain,
% where"; with more than about ten rows, or when the message is a systematic shift, use parity.tex instead.
